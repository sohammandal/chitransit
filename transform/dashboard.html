A trip is a silent ghost only after the innocent explanations are ruled out: no bus reported it (matched on pattern and start, or route and start for detours, including a bus on its pattern under another trip key); the feed was swept for ≥ 90% of it; no detour ran on the route within the hour; its block's bus did not vanish and reappear at the trip's far end (a dead tracker); CTA's predictions at one of its own stops were sampled before it was due and never flagged it. Strict counts only ghosts with physical evidence the bus didn't run it: it was seen doing something else during the trip, or reappeared where the trip started. Lenient counts all. Missing: the same, but its stops weren't sampled, so we can't say whether CTA announced it. Delivered = ran ÷ (ran + announced + silent + missing). Bunched: headway under 25% of scheduled; gaps: over 2×; as a share of stop passes, terminals excluded.
